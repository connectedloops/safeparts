---
title: Desktop applications retired
description: Recover with supported tools after retirement of the Tauri, native macOS, and native Windows applications.
---

The Tauri desktop app, native SwiftUI macOS app, and native WinUI Windows app are retired. They are no longer supported, verified, or packaged for future releases. Their source and dedicated UniFFI bridge remain dormant reference code. No replacement application or timeline is promised.

Historical releases remain available, but their GUI installers are unsupported. This notice does not update or fix applications you already downloaded.

## Keep your Recovery shares

Retirement does not invalidate existing Recovery shares. Keep your saved shares and any passphrases used for Passphrase protection. Do not delete or overwrite them as part of changing tools.

1. Choose a supported recovery tool:
   - Use the [web app](../web-ui/) for UTF-8 text with `base64url` or `mnemo-words` shares.
   - Use the [CLI](../cli/) or [TUI](../tui/) for all four Share encodings and file recovery that preserves the exact bytes. CLI/TUI archives remain available for Linux, Windows, and macOS through the [download guide](../build-and-run/).
2. Gather at least the Threshold number of Recovery shares from the same set in a controlled recovery environment. Supply the original passphrase if Passphrase protection was used. Keep Secrets, shares, and passphrases out of online converters and logs.
3. Verify recovery with the supported tool before relying on it. Follow the [saved-backup checkpoint](../it-devops-guide/break-glass/#verify-saved-backup) to check your actual saved copies, compare exact bytes, and restore separated custody after the check. A synthetic practice run alone does not verify your saved shares.

If recovery fails, retain the original shares and passphrase records and consult [troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/). Do not replace them with incomplete or altered output.
